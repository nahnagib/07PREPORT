# How to fill the MARCOM template (version 2.0) — for the Marketing Manager

**Every month:** download a fresh template from *MARCOM Data Upload → Download template*. It already lists our brands and has Year / Month / Brand / Platform filled in on Page 3 — you only type numbers.

**Colours:** 🟨 Yellow = you type · ⬜ Grey = calculated, locked · 🟩 Green row = example (leave it, it is never imported).
**Rules:** monthly figures only — **never YTD** (the dashboard adds the months up). Amounts in LYD. Type numbers only: no text, spaces or line breaks (do not paste from Numbers / WhatsApp). Pick Month, Brand, Platform and Category from the dropdowns. Clicking a yellow header shows its definition.
**BMH** is not a brand: use it for **group events**. BMH rows have spend only — leave the revenue columns empty.

## Page 1 – MARCOM Spending (one row per Brand per Month)

| Column | What to type |
|---|---|
| Brand Revenue – Current Month | Sales of **this brand's products only** that month. Example: in September Tika sold 8,000,000 in total, Xtreme products were 3,000,000 → type **3,000,000** on the Xtreme row. |
| Brand Revenue – Same Month Last Year | The same figure for the same month last year (e.g. Xtreme, September last year). |
| Revenue Attributed to Marketing | The part of Brand Revenue that came from marketing: campaign codes, promo sales, tracked leads. **Never more than Brand Revenue.** |
| Company Total Revenue – Current Month *(optional)* | The whole company's sales that month (Tika 8,000,000). Only used for "Brand share of company". Leave empty if you don't have it. |
| Budget Allocated | The MARCOM budget planned for this brand and month (a real amount, not a % or a formula). |
| Finance Booked MARCOM Total *(optional)* | The total MARCOM cost finance booked for this brand and month. The sheet compares it with Campaign + Other spend; any difference shows red in **Unallocated**. |
| # of New Customers | New customers this month for this brand. |
| Avg Sales per Invoice | Average invoice value (LYD) this month. |
| Total Cost of Social Media Posts | What the brand's social posts cost this month. |
| Total Clicks | Clicks on those posts this month. |

You do **not** type Total MARCOM Spend: it is calculated as **Campaign Spend** (from Page 2, split over the months each campaign runs) **+ Other MARCOM Spend** (from Page 1b).

## Page 1b – Other MARCOM Spending (one row per expense that is not a campaign)

Year · Month · Brand · **Category** (Uniforms, Printing & Printouts, Gifts & Giveaways, Events & Sponsorships, Branding & Signage, POS / Merchandising Materials, Photography & Production, Agency / Freelancer Fees, Other) · **Description** (what was bought, e.g. "Staff polo shirts – showroom team"; don't repeat the same description twice in one month/brand/category) · Vendor *(optional)* · **Amount (LYD)** · Notes *(optional)*.
Example: total MARCOM for the month 1,000,000 → 4 campaigns 600,000 (Page 2) + the other 400,000 here (uniforms, printouts, gifts, sponsorship…). Unallocated then shows 0.

## Page 2 – Media Campaigns (unchanged)

One row per campaign: Brand, Start / End Date, Status, Total Spend, Revenue Attributed to Campaign. Table B: OOH units and cost per campaign, media type and city.

## Page 3 – Digital Performance (per Brand)

**Table A – Social media:** one row per **Year + Month + Brand + Platform**. Xtreme's Facebook and Majaal's Facebook are separate rows. Fill *Account / Page Name* only if one brand has two pages on the same platform (e.g. "Xtreme Libya FB" and "Xtreme Benghazi FB"). Followers = the count at the **end** of the month. Paid / Organic Engagement = likes + comments + shares + saves + clicks; type 0 only if there really were none.
**Table B – Website:** one row per **Year + Month + Brand** (each brand has its own website): bounce visitors, total visitors, sessions, total minutes.
Rows you don't fill (pre-filled months/platforms) are simply ignored.

## Page 4 – Trade Marketing (unchanged)

## Before you upload

The upload screen checks the file and tells you the cell of each problem. It **blocks** the upload for: a missing brand revenue, attributed revenue above brand revenue, brand revenue above company total, missing Brand on Page 3, the same brand + platform twice in a month, an unknown category. It **warns** (you may still upload) when a value is more than 5× that brand's usual month (an extra zero?), when two brands have identical figures (copy-paste?), when one value repeats in every row (e.g. the same budget everywhere), or when brand revenue equals company total.
