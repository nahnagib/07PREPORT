# Getting started

## What you need

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Web browser | A current version of **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** (recommended), **Mozilla Firefox** or **Safari**. Keep your browser up to date. |
| Screen | A laptop or desktop screen (1280 pixels wide or more) gives the best view. The dashboard also works on tablets and phones, with the menus rearranged to fit. |
| Network | Internet access to `https://dashboards.benmussaholding.com/Dashboard` |
| Account | A user account created by the system administrator. There is no self-registration. |
| PDF reader | Any PDF viewer (for example your browser or Adobe Acrobat Reader) to open exported reports. |

## Opening the dashboard

1. Open your web browser.
2. Go to **https://dashboards.benmussaholding.com/Dashboard**
3. If you are not signed in, the **Sign in** page opens.

> [!TIP]
> Add the address to your browser's bookmarks or favourites so you can open it in one click.

> [!TIP]
> You can also install the dashboard as an app on your computer or phone, so it opens from its own icon. For a short video showing how to do this, contact support (see *Support contact*).

## Signing in

![The Sign in page](shots/login.png){annot="1:505,228;2:505,305;3:505,362;4:372,411" width="55%"}

1. **Email**: type the email address of your dashboard account.
2. **Password**: type your password. The characters are hidden as you type.
3. Click **Sign in**. The button shows **Signing in...** while your details are checked.
4. **Forgot your password?**: see *Forgotten password* below.

### Sign-in messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Invalid email or password.** | The email or the password is wrong. | Check the spelling, the **Caps Lock** key and the keyboard language, then try again. |
| **Your account is locked. Contact your administrator.** | Your account has been locked, for example after 5 wrong passwords in a row, or by an administrator. | Contact the administrator (see *Support*). Only the administrator can unlock it. |
| **Your account is inactive. Contact your administrator.** | Your account has been switched off. | Contact the administrator. |
| **Too many attempts. Please try again later.** | Too many sign-in attempts were made from your connection in a short time (more than 10 in 15 minutes). | Wait 15 minutes, then try again. |
| **Something went wrong. Please try again.** | A temporary network or server problem. | Check your internet connection and try again. If it continues, contact support. |

> [!WARNING]
> After **5 wrong passwords in a row**, your account is locked automatically. If you are not sure of your password, stop and contact the administrator before you are locked out.

## First-time sign-in: setting your own password

New accounts are created with a temporary password. The first time you sign in, the dashboard asks you to replace it before you can open any report.

![The password form. On first sign-in it is titled Set a new password and also shows Sign out instead.](shots/change_password.png){annot="1:420,197;2:420,274;3:420,373;4:420,430" width="70%" r="12"}

1. **Current password**: type the temporary password you were given.
2. **New password**: type a new password. It must be **at least 8 characters long** and contain **at least one letter and one number**.
3. **Confirm new password**: type the new password again, exactly the same.
4. Click **Save new password**. The dashboard opens.

On first sign-in, the form is titled **Set a new password** and has a **Sign out instead** link under the button. That link leaves without changing the password, and you'll be asked again next time.

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **New password and confirmation do not match.** | Type the same new password in both boxes. |
| **Password must be at least 8 characters long.** | Use a longer password. |
| **Password must contain at least one letter and one number.** | Add a letter or a number. |
| **Current password is incorrect.** | Re-type the temporary password exactly as you received it. |

> [!TIP]
> Choose a password you have not used elsewhere, and do not share it. Your data access is personal to your account.

## Forgotten password

Password resets need **administrator confirmation**.

1. Contact the system administrator (see *Support contact*) and ask for a password reset for your account email.
2. When the administrator has confirmed the reset, you'll receive a temporary password or a reset link.
3. Sign in with it. The dashboard then asks you to set a new password, as described in *First-time sign-in*.

> [!NOTE]
> The Sign in page also has a **Forgot your password?** link. Don't use it: always contact the administrator to reset your password.

## Changing your password

To change your password, follow the same process as for a forgotten password:

1. Contact the system administrator (see *Support contact*) and ask for a password change for your account email.
2. When the administrator has confirmed the change, you'll receive a temporary password or a reset link.
3. Sign in with it. The dashboard then asks you to set a new password, as described in *First-time sign-in*.

> [!NOTE]
> Changing your password signs you out on your other devices and browsers. Sign in there again with the new password.

## Signing out

1. Click the **Log out** icon (an arrow leaving a door) at the far right of the header.
2. The **Sign in** page opens. Your session on this browser is ended.

> [!WARNING]
> Always sign out on a shared or public computer. If you only close the browser tab, you stay signed in on that computer.

## Session timeout (how long you stay signed in)

> [!NOTE]
> **The dashboard has no automatic time-out for inactivity.** After you sign in, you stay signed in on that browser, even if you close it and come back days later, until one of these happens:
>
> - you click **Log out**;
> - your password is changed (you are signed out everywhere else);
> - the administrator resets your password, locks your account or ends your sessions;
> - 365 days have passed since you signed in.
>
> If your session ends while you are working, the page shows **Your session has expired.** or returns to **Sign in**. Just sign in again. Your filters go back to their defaults.
