# Reports and exporting

Every report is generated on screen, live, from the filters you choose. To keep or share a report, export it.

## Export types

| Button | Where you find it | File you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Export PDF** / **Export as PDF** | Next to **Performance Details** tables and other detail tables | A PDF of the table, including every row (on several pages if needed). The first page records the **filters applied** and who exported it (**Exported by**: your email). |
| **Export CSV** | On large tables, such as the Tachometer **Full Breakdown**<!-- only:full --> and the BCG Matrix **Product Detail**<!-- /only --> | A CSV file (comma-separated values) that opens in Microsoft Excel. Arabic and special characters are kept. |
<!-- only:full -->
| **Export image** | On chart cards | A PNG picture of the chart, ready to paste into a presentation or email. |
| **Download as PDF** | On drill-down lists (for example Pipeline Health opportunity details) | A PDF of the list. |
<!-- /only -->

## Exporting a report, step by step

1. Open the report.
2. Set the filters you need (see *The filter bar*). **The export contains exactly what the report shows for those filters**, including the date range.
3. Scroll to the table<!-- only:full --> or chart<!-- /only --> you want.
4. Click **Export PDF**<!-- only:full -->, **Export as PDF**, **Export CSV** or **Export image**<!-- /only --><!-- only:sales --> or **Export CSV**<!-- /only -->.
5. While the file is prepared, the button may show **Exporting...**. Wait until it finishes.
6. Your browser saves the file.

The file name describes the content. For example, a YTD Value breakdown by salesperson is saved as `ytdValue-breakdown-by-salesperson.csv`.

## Where exported files are saved

Files are saved to your browser's **Downloads** folder, normally **This PC › Downloads** on Windows or **Downloads** on a Mac.

- **Chrome / Edge:** press **Ctrl + J** (Windows) or **⌘ + Shift + J** (Mac) to see recent downloads, then click **Show in folder**.
- If your browser is set to "Ask where to save each file", it asks you to choose a folder first.

## Printing

The dashboard has no Print button of its own. To print a report:

1. Export it with **Export PDF** / **Export as PDF**.
2. Open the PDF from your **Downloads** folder.
3. Print it from your PDF viewer (**Ctrl + P** / **⌘ + P**).

> [!TIP]
> To print a whole screen as it looks, switch to light mode first, then use your browser's **Print** (**Ctrl + P**) and choose **Landscape**. For tables, the PDF export gives a cleaner result.

> [!WARNING]
> Exported files contain company sales data. Store them only in approved company locations and don't send them outside the company.
