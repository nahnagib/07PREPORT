# Introduction

## What the 7Ps Dashboard is for

The **BMH 7Ps Dashboard** is Ben Mussa Holding's business-intelligence website. It turns sales, pipeline, marketing and product data from the company's systems into clear, up-to-date reports you can open in a web browser, with nothing to install.

The dashboard is organised around the **7Ps** of the marketing mix. Each "P" is a department:

| Department ("P") | Business area | Status at launch |
|---|---|---|
| **Promotion** | Commercial Department (sales performance) | Live |
<!-- only:full -->
| **Product** | Production Department (product portfolio and stock) | Live |
<!-- /only -->
| **Price** | Finance Department | Coming Soon |
| **Place** | Supply Chain Department | Coming Soon |
| **People** | Human Resource Department | Coming Soon |
| **Process** | Excellence Department | Coming Soon |
| **Physical Evidence** | HSE Department | Coming Soon |

<!-- only:full -->
At launch, the **Promotion** department has 12 reports and the **Product** department has 4. All of them are described in this manual.
<!-- /only -->
<!-- only:sales -->
As a salesperson, you use the **Tachometer** report in the Promotion department. It shows how your own sales compare with your targets, year-to-date and month-to-date.
<!-- /only -->

## Who this manual is for

<!-- only:full -->
This manual is for **full-access users**: managers, directors and analysts who can open every report in the Promotion and Product departments. It covers the whole journey, from signing in to reading each report, filtering the data and exporting results.

<!-- /only -->
<!-- only:sales -->
This manual is for **salespersons**. Your account can open one report, the **Tachometer**, and it always shows **your own sales only**. This manual covers everything you need: signing in, reading the Tachometer, changing the dates, opening the detailed breakdown, exporting and printing, and signing out.
<!-- /only -->

You don't need any technical knowledge to use the dashboard or this manual.

## How to use this manual

- Button names, menu items, field names and messages are written in **bold**, exactly as they appear on screen. For example: click **Sign in**.
- Step-by-step procedures are numbered. Follow the steps in order.
- Numbered red circles on a screenshot match the numbered list under it.
- Screenshots use sample data: names and figures have been replaced, so the numbers you see on your screen will differ.

Three kinds of boxes highlight important information:

> [!NOTE]
> Extra information that helps you understand how something works.

> [!TIP]
> A shortcut or a better way to do something.

> [!WARNING]
> Something that could cause a problem or an unexpected result if you ignore it.
